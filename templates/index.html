Analysis Rules
Recommendations use scored confluence of market rules plus 5 strategy methods: EMA trend (price vs EMA20/EMA50, ±2) · RSI momentum (≥60 / ≤40, ±1) · Futures OI buildup (long/short buildup ±2, covering/unwinding ±1) · PCR (>1.2 / <0.8, ±1) · Futures basis (premium/discount >0.2%, ±1) · Day momentum (>±0.75%, ±1).
Method 1 — Moving Average: price above both EMA20 & EMA50 → Buy CE; below both → Buy PE. EMA 20/50 Crossover: EMA20 crossing above EMA50 → Buy CE (+2); crossing below → Buy PE (−2). Supertrend + EMA20 Cross: close crossing above both Supertrend and EMA20 on the same candle → Buy CE (+2); crossing below both → Buy PE (−2). Method 2 — Support & Resistance: break above resistance / below support on ≥1.2× average volume (±2). Method 3 — Risk-Reward: Risk = Entry − SL, Reward = Target − Entry; trade only if Reward ÷ Risk ≥ 2 (target +60%, SL −30% ⇒ 1:2). Method 4 — Option Delta: Δ ≈ 0.50 = ATM; CE Δ ≥ 0.60 stronger CE (+1); PE Δ ≤ −0.60 stronger PE (−1). Quick Formula: price > VWAP + RSI > 60 + volume rising → Buy CE; price < VWAP + RSI < 40 + volume rising → Buy PE (±2).
Score ≥ +3 → BUY CE (≥ +6 high conviction) · Score ≤ −3 → BUY PE (≤ −6 high conviction) · otherwise WAIT. Educational only — not investment advice.

How these picks work
Each session (09:15–11:45 · 11:45–14:00 · 14:00–15:30) is scored from today's price move inside that window (±2) and how the same window behaved on recent days (±1). Score ≥ +1 → BUY CE · ≤ −1 → BUY PE · else WAIT. Strike = ATM · target +40% / stop-loss −20% of premium (1:2 risk-reward) · sell by the session end time (or earlier at target/SL).
